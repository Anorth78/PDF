SCORM TRANSLATION COMPATIBILITY & GUIDELINES
A Quick Reference Guide for Digital Content Creators & Instructional Designers
Impact of Language Expansion: Translated content (e.g., German, French, Spanish) typically expands by 20% to 40%
compared to source English text. Following the tool-specific layout guidelines below prevents visual overlapping, truncation, and
costly manual post-translation adjustments.
1. Articulate Storyline HIGH COMPATIBILITY
CAPABILITIES LIMITATIONS & RISKS AUTHORING GUIDELINES
Automatic Scroll Bars: When
"Accessible Text" is enabled,
Storyline dynamically adds scroll
bars to text boxes if translated
content expands beyond bounds. 
When Accessible Text is OFF, text
expansion causes awkward line wraps,
overlapping elements, or truncation outside
the visible slide area. 
Enable Accessible Text: Always turn on
Accessible Text in player settings to allow
dynamic scrolling for expanded text.
Allow Padding: Leave extra space
around buttons and text boxes.
2. Articulate Rise MODERATE-HIGH COMPATIBILITY
CAPABILITIES LIMITATIONS & RISKS AUTHORING GUIDELINES
Responsive Layout: Content flows
vertically, accommodating long
translated sentences in web-based
block formats. 
Section headers and structured cards/grids
can experience overlapping text or severe
line clipping if margins are tight. 
Header Vertical Space: Insert a blank
line (using Enter) below headers to
provide vertical buffer space.
Reduce Bullet Font Size: Slightly
decrease bullet point font sizes to absorb
20-40% text expansion smoothly.
3. Adobe Captivate REQUIRES HIGH LAYOUT CARE
CAPABILITIES LIMITATIONS & RISKS AUTHORING GUIDELINES
Supports high-design custom
layouts and precise positioning for
multi-device delivery. 
Fixed-Height Containers: Tightly fitted or
fixed boxes result in severe truncation, text
cutoff, and broken slide graphics. 
White Space Padding: Provide
generous white space around titles,
headings, and captions.
Flexible Containers: Enable auto-sizing/
responsive text boxes; avoid fixed
heights.
Font Scaling: Use smaller font sizes for
list items/bullets.

CRITICAL UNIVERSAL RULE: AVOID EMBEDDED TEXT IN IMAGES
Never embed translatable text inside image files (JPG, PNG, GIF). Embedded text cannot be automatically extracted or translated by
translation tools, requiring manual graphic redesign. Always use editable floating text layers/objects over background images to
support automated translation pipelines.
