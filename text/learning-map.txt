S7 Adjacency — Assistant, IQ, and 
Splunk
8 min • Differentiate AI Canvas vs AI Assistant (workspace vs chat, 
multiplayer vs private)
• Position Cloud Control vs IQ as complementary: live ops vs 
lifecycle/risk; same asset truth
• Position AI Canvas + Splunk: Canvas coordinates; Splunk deepens 
historical analysis

S8 Four money plays — what's in it for 
partners
12 min • Lead the unified operations conversation (boardroom, not BOM)
• Drive pull-through: expand, displace, accelerate the second and third 
product; value compounds with each connected product
• Build services around agentic operations: Canvas = resolution; Studio 
= build (domain agents, workflow packs, integration apps, design)
• Extend value through Marketplace: services today, publish IP over 
time — resale to co-creation

S9 Pitch practice — 30 seconds, 3 
minutes, 30 minutes
10 min • Deliver a 30-second elevator using: AgenticOps is the model; Cloud 
Control is the harness

Pre-Sales Stage 1 overview
• Black Belt Cisco Cloud Control Pre-Sales Stage 1 (Sep 2026)
• Cisco Cloud Control-Pre-Sales-Stage 1   ·   128 min   ·   13 modules   ·   4 shared
