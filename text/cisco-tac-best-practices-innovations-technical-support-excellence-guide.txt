What to include in the case? 
✓ Detailed & Technical Problem Description
✓ Urgency and business impact of the 
problem & deadlines (if any)
✓ Software Version of affected product
✓ Hardware Platform and Serial Number
✓ Topology of the network
✓ Trigger (if any)
✓ Troubleshooting already done (if any)
✓ Technical logs from the system
✓ Logs from monitoring systems (if any)
✓ Time of first occurance of the issue
✓ Up-to-date contact information

Severity 1
▪ Production network down
▪ Critical impact to business operations
▪ 24-hour Cisco, Partner and Customer commitment
Severity 2
▪ Network severely degraded
▪ Significant impact to business operations
▪ Cisco, Partner and Customer committed during business hours
Severity 3
▪ Network functionality degraded
▪ Business operations noticeably impaired
▪ Cisco, Partner and Customer in frequent contact
Severity 4
▪ General assistance – Ask a question 
▪ Installation, upgrade, or configuration assistance
▪ General product information

RMA – Customer Handoff
• When RMA is needed system will send a handoff email to the SR requester or 
Acknowledgment contact 
• Click on “Complete your RMA” button in the email. This will take you to review 
& submit page on the RMA tool. 
• Review all details including SITE Address before clicking Submit, to initiate 
delivery of the RMA. 
• If you do not submit the draft RMA, you will get subsequent reminder emails to 
complete your draft RMA. 
• Draft RMAs auto expire if action is not taken within 7 days of initiation

General Guidelines
• One case in T AC = one problem. Do not mix different problems in one case.
• If you encounter new problems in ongoing Service Request, the T AC engineer will advise
to open separate case.
• TAC is ”break/fix” support (day2).  For design/implementation (day0/1) requests, you 
will be advised to reach out to the appropriate Account T eam regarding Professional 
Services.
• You are in control of Service Request’sSeverity. 
• If Business Impact of the issue changed and you wish to change to Severity 2 or Severity 
1, it is best practice to do it over the phone, not e-mail.
