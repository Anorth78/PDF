What is 3 Way RMA: 
The 3-Way RMA enables Partners to use spare 
inventory in their depot during RMA 
replacements. When a device becomes faulty at 
the customer site, the Partner can replace the 
faulty device with their spare part, and Cisco will 
provide a replacement for the failed part, which 
can then be kept as a spare again.
How to register? 
Benefits of 3 Way RMA sparing process: 
• Allows undisrupted service for end customer 
without any delays with the spare item 
available in partner depot
• Automated RMA process supporting 3 Way 
RMA transactions
• Minimize / eliminate partner overhead to 
monitor and coordinate contract swaps.
Pre-conditions for successful 3 Way / Self-
Sparing RMA processing:
• This applies only for partner branded 
services. 
• Partner needs to be registered for 3 Way 
RMA / Self-Sparing transactions
• Defective SN (Serial Number) matches the 
Product ID (PID) and is actively covered on 
a Partner branded service contract.
Partners- 
• Partner needs to be registered for 3 Way RMA / Self-Sparing transactions using the link 
• Partner need to work with respective Cisco contacts (POM’s, Service entitlement leader, Sales or any of the Cisco contacts) for details on the 
registration process (Bill to ID, customer name, cco id details, Partner BEGEO ID etc)
