Complementary
Cloud Control connects Cisco operations. AI Canvas 
and Assistant coordinate the investigations. Splunk 
adds deeper evidence across systems and time.
Customers connect broad analytical insight directly 
to the affected Cisco environment and the response.
Supplementary
Cloud Control delivers investigation and response 
value on its own. Splunk expands that value with 
historical context, cross

 system correlation, and 
reusable analytics.
Customers gain more confidence in the diagnosis,

worked.
Redundant

findings, visualize results, recommend actions, or 
start workflows. The overlap supports flexibility, but it 
requires clear ownership.
Customers can work in the experience that best fits 
the task without creating conflicting answers or 
duplicate actions.
