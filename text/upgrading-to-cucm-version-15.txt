Direct Upgrade vs Refresh Upgrade
BRKCOL-2082 12
Direct Upgrade
Refresh Upgrade
• Active partition is running while software is upgraded on inactive partition
• Low complexity with shortest possible downtime
• Formerly called L2 Upgrade
• Server is down while upgrade is taking place on inactive partition
• Medium complexity with longer downtime
