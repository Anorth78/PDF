Executive Brief — Cisco Cloud Control 
The Strategic Context: The Operating Model is Changing 
Customers' work is growing faster than the teams they can staff for it, and product-by-product management 
no longer scales. Cisco Cloud Control introduces a unified, AI-native operating model — AgenticOps — where 
people and AI agents work across product boundaries under clear human accountability. It aggregates the 
controllers customers already run rather than replacing them. 
Where we are: GA in the United States since August 25, 2026 — more than 300 customers in production, 
and a global waitlist that passed 5,000 before launch. This is a deployment conversation, not a launch 
announcement. 
Value to the Customer: Unified, AI-Native Operations at Scale 
• Structural Advantage: Alerts aggregated and correlated, cross-domain visibility, and AI-assisted 
investigation across Networking, Collaboration, Compute, Security, Observability, and Services — 
while each product keeps its specialized controls. 
• Rapid Resolution: AI Canvas puts specialists and AI agents in one multiplayer workspace. In a live 
session, a global supply chain software company resolved in under ten minutes what had taken over 
ten hours. 
• No New License, No New Cost: Included in the Essential and Advantage tiers of eligible Cisco 
subscriptions, so customers start without a separate budget cycle or business case. 
• The CIO Hook: "How quickly can you move from alert to resolution across your entire Cisco estate?"

Use the partner study first. Unlike the market table below, Cisco's 2026 Global AI Partner Study is first-party, 
public, partner-specific, and safe to quote from the stage — 1,702 partners across 30 countries, published 
September 2026. Consulting and advisory services jumped from 12% to 50% as a leading growth opportunity, the 
largest increase of any category. 92% of partners report customers raising concerns about AI running costs, while 
only 43% say customer ROI is meeting or exceeding expectations. Nearly half of customers are piloting or scaling 
agentic AI, and multi-agent orchestration ranked as the weakest capability measured across customer readiness, 
partner capability, knowledge, and hands-on experience. Roughly a quarter of partners say AI already makes up 
more than half their revenue, and 55% expect to be there within five years.

A3 — What MSP partners can package and sell 
The GA Ideal Partner Profile includes single-tenant MSPs. Cloud Control aligns with recurring managed 
operations for that profile: delegated tenancy, co-managed NOC/SOC, managed automation, compliance, 
lifecycle assurance, and vertical service packs. 
These are services a partner packages and sells around Cloud Control — not Cloud Control features. Like the ISV 
motion in A4, treat this as forward-looking. The GA tenant capability is multiple product tenants for a single 
customer (see the tenant Q&A), not partner multi-tenancy.

A4 — ISV motion, adjacent to the Ideal Partner Profile 
ISVs are not in the GA Ideal Partner Profile. The profile remains U.S.-based Cisco Preferred Partners (PVI 
7.5+) — VARs, SIs, GSIs, and single-tenant MSPs. The ISV motion is a strong current opportunity adjacent to 
that gate, and it is forward-looking. 
Build: Cloud Control-aware agents, workflow packs, integrations, and domain apps. 
Sell: architecture and design services around data models, RBAC, governance, and workflows.
