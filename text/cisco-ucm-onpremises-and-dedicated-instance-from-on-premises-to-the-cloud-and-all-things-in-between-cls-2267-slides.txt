Why migrate to 15 ?
1. Mature (SU3a), the most secure, with long-term support.
2. Fixes CentOS EOL.
• V12.5 went end of TAC support Aug 31, 2005.
• V14 goes end of bug/security fix in April CY26.
• No extended maintenance (rising dependency problems with old database, runtimes, 
security modules, etc. … just like 11.5 back during Covid).
3. Fixes VMware by Broadcom.
• Exiting?  15 SU4 required for ESXi alternatives.
• Staying? 15 SU4 required for ESXi 9.0.  12.5 can’t upgrade to 8.0+.
4. Eventually required for hardware refresh.

Do what the Upgrade Readiness COP files say!
Expect to iterate:
• Run, get output file.  Fix any CRIT, FAIL.  WARN might need fix or be ok.
• Re-run until all checks are PASS or explainable WARN.
• After upgrade, run post-upgrade-COP (different file) for before/after comparison of certain items.
FAIL on legacy vdisk layouts, guest OS partitions, swap or certain ext3 means you 
must either:
• Direct Migrate to 15
• Backup/Restore all nodes, then you can Direct Upgrade to 12.5
• No shortcuts/hacks!
