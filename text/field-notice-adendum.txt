© 2018  Cisco and/or its affiliates. All rights reserved.   Cisco Confidential
Operational Insights Portal
Offers two options for data download 
• Export PDF: entire dashboard can be exported into a 
PDF. Option available at top right corner of the screen.
• Export Results: Each widget results can exported into 
CSV, JSON or XML format. Option available under each 
widget on hover.
Note: It is advised to leverage Excel based NP report for 
more detailed data download.
Network profiler
Suitable for detailed reports. To generate report:
• NP Home page -> Reports -> Schedule Reports
• Select deliverable under ‘Reports to generate’, 
respective parameters, click Next until you get Create 
Report option.

Field Notices Addendum 2 : Daily Syslog Report
© 2018  Cisco and/or its affiliates. All rights reserved.   Cisco Confidential
Overview
• An email notification about the syslog's which are of interest to the 
customer.
• One fundamental component is the IC catalogue. There is a default 
Cisco catalogue that evolves over-time. Company-specific catalogue 
can also be developed. When both catalogues exist, the customer-
specific catalogue takes precedence.
Report generation
To configure Syslog Reports, perform the following:
• On the Insights screen, a Gear     Icon is available on the top right 
corner of the screen. Select Syslog Reporting from the menu 
options listed under the icon.
• On Syslog Report generation screen, select Meta data of the report 
i.e. who gets it, how frequently, what is the scope of the report and so 
on, and click on Update to register for automated syslog reporting.
You can leverage Help button on the top right corner of the screen to find 
more details on the Syslog Report generation process..
Note: The ‘Group’ filter is available only under ‘Syslog Report’. It is 
NOT available on the Insights Portal under ‘Diagnostic Analysis’.
